# Get-in & identity journeys — live measurement (`manual_user_check`)

Cluster: `SUBTASK_getin_identity`. Measures the 22 `manual_user_check` `BAR-*` assigned to this cluster
against the chirp product at `Nousix-LLC/nswe-demo` commit `c805525`. One row per owned `BAR-*`, keyed by
`bar_id`, with exactly one `live_status` ∈ {met, partial, missing, not_applicable} and a grounded observation.

## Access note

The live product was reached and exercised read-only. Both surfaces were available at `c805525`: the
single-page app loaded (its wasm bundle served) and the JSON API answered. Identity surfaces were walked with
a single throwaway test account doing only its own normal actions (one sign-up, one handle-only login, one
probe chirp); nothing else was mutated. Where the running UI was thin or ambiguous, presence or absence was
confirmed against the SPA/API source at `c805525` (repo-relative paths cited below): the server route table
(`crates/chirp-server/src/api/router.rs`), the handlers (`crates/chirp-server/src/api/handlers.rs`), the
in-memory repository (`crates/chirp-server/src/repository/in_memory.rs`), the shared wire types
(`crates/chirp-types/src/api.rs`, `crates/chirp-types/src/domain.rs`), and the SPA composition root and views
(`crates/chirp-frontend/src/app.rs`, `.../compose.rs`). The product at `c805525` runs on an in-memory store
and omits whole release-layer surfaces; the credential, verified-contact, recovery, session-lifecycle, and
signed-out/get-in screens are the relevant omissions and are confirmed below, not presumed.

The Phase-B `design` methodology loader was unavailable run-wide (`agent_domain_unset`); this measurement
applies the principal-product-designer persona and the relayed ReleaseBar guidance and proceeds, as the
subtree hub directs.
