`live_status` from `{met, partial, missing, not_applicable}` and an observation recording what was exercised or

## Access note

- **Live product reached.** The deployed product at `c805525` was reached and exercised read-only. Both the
  single-page app and the JSON API answered, and the running service was confirmed to be the `c805525` build.
  User-journey checks used throwaway test accounts doing only their own ordinary actions. The one operator-style
  action was a restart of the project's own serving workload, made to observe whether state survives a restart.
- **Repository inspected.** The repository test suite at `c805525` was built and run with its own toolchain (all native
  tests passed; browser-gated tests are compile-only and were not executed). Config, manifests, and code were read
  from the checkout at `c805525`.
- **Limits.** Where the compiled single-page app could not be driven to settle a verdict, the surface's presence or
  absence was confirmed against the app and API source at `c805525` and cited by repo-relative path. Criteria that
  could not be observed directly were judged conservatively, with the reason in the observation. Surfaces that do not
  exist at `c805525` are recorded as `missing`, not `not_applicable`.
- **Methodology loader.** The `design` methodology loader was unavailable for this run; the principal-product-designer
  persona and the release-bar guidance were applied instead.
